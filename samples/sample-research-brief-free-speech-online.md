## What the law says

### Anuradha Bhasin v. Union of India (2020) 3 SCC 637 — Internet access and proportionality

Citation: Constitution of India, 1950, Case note — Anuradha Bhasin v. Union of India (2020) 3 SCC 637.
Source: https://www.juristco.com/ (summary verified against JuristCo; judgment: Supreme Court of India)

## Case note (verified)

In *Anuradha Bhasin v. Union of India* (2020) 3 SCC 637, the
Supreme Court held that **access to the internet is protected under
Article 19(1)(a)** (freedom of speech and expression). Any
restriction on internet access — including a shutdown — must satisfy
the **test of proportionality**: it must be necessary, and no wider
than needed to achieve its aim.

### Constitution of India — Article 19: Protection of certain rights regarding freedom of speech, etc.

Citation: Constitution of India, 1950, Article 19(1)(a) and Article 19(2).
Source: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/ (India Code); official English text via https://www.legalserviceindia.com/constitution/const_india.htm

## Statutory text (verbatim extract)

Article 19(1):

> "All citizens shall have the right — (a) to freedom of speech and expression; (b) to assemble peaceably and without arms; (c) to form associations or unions; (d) to move freely throughout the territory of India; (e) to reside and settle in any part of the territory of India; (g) to practise any profession, or to carry on any occupation, trade or business."

(Sub-clause (f) — the right to acquire, hold and dispose of property — was omitted by the Constitution (44th Amendment) Act, 1978.)

Article 19(2):

> "Nothing in sub-clause (a) of clause (1) shall affect the operation of any existing law, or prevent the State from making any law, in so far as such law imposes reasonable restrictions on the exercise of the right conferred by the said sub-clause in the interests of the sovereignty and integrity of India, the security of the State, friendly relations with foreign States, public order, decency or morality, or in relation to contempt of court, defamation or incitement to an offence."
