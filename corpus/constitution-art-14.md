# Constitution of India — Article 14: Equality before law

Act: Constitution of India, 1950
Section: Article 14 (Part III — Fundamental Rights)
Source: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/ (India Code); reference copy of the Legislative Department text at https://www.aipgp.in/constitution

#constitution #fundamental-rights #equality

## Statutory text (verbatim extract)

> "The State shall not deny to any person equality before the law or the equal protection of the laws within the territory of India."

## Plain-language note (summary, not the law)

Article 14 is in Part III (Fundamental Rights) of the Constitution.
It binds the State. It requires the State to treat equals equally,
and it permits reasonable classification — different treatment where
there is a real, intelligible difference and a rational link to the
law's purpose. It does not require identical treatment of people in
different situations.

## Why this provision is in the corpus

The starting point for any equality or discrimination question, and
the Article that the Supreme Court reads together with Articles 19
and 21.
