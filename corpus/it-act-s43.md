# Information Technology Act, 2000 — Section 43: Penalty and compensation for damage to computer, computer system, etc.

Act: Information Technology Act, 2000 (Act 21 of 2000)
Section: Section 43 (Chapter IX — Penalties and Adjudication)
Source: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/ (India Code); bare Act text at https://www.courtkutchehry.com/bare-acts/information-technology-act-2000/

#it-act #cyber #civil-liability #hacking #compensation

## Statutory text (summary of the provision, verified)

Section 43 imposes **civil liability** on any person who, without
the permission of the owner or person in charge of a computer,
computer system or computer network: accesses or secures access to
it; downloads, copies or extracts any data; introduces a computer
contaminant or virus; damages or disrupts it; denies access to an
authorised person; charges services to another person; or destroys,
deletes or alters information in it.

The person is liable to pay **damages by way of compensation** to
the person affected, **not exceeding one crore rupees (₹1 crore)**.

## Plain-language note (summary, not the law)

Section 43 is the civil side of unauthorised computer use: the
victim can claim money. It is distinct from the criminal offence in
section 66, which punishes the same acts when done dishonestly or
fraudulently. A single act can attract both: compensation under
s. 43 and prosecution under s. 66.

## Why this provision is in the corpus

The civil remedy for hacking, unauthorised access, data theft and
damage to a computer system — the money claim behind a cybercrime
complaint.
