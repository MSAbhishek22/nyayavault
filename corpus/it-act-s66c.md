# Information Technology Act, 2000 — Section 66C: Punishment for identity theft

Act: Information Technology Act, 2000 (Act 21 of 2000)
Section: Section 66C (Chapter XI — Offences)
Source: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/ (India Code); bare Act text at https://www.courtkutchehry.com/bare-acts/information-technology-act-2000/

#it-act #cyber #identity-theft #password #impersonation

## Statutory text (verbatim extract)

> "Whoever, fraudulently or dishonestly make use of the electronic signature, password or any other unique identification feature of any other person, shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to three years and shall also be liable to fine which may extend to rupees one lakh."

## Plain-language note (summary, not the law)

Section 66C is the identity-theft section. Using someone else's
password, OTP, electronic signature or other unique identifier —
for example, to take money from a bank account — is an offence,
even if no money was taken. The punishment is imprisonment up to
three years and a fine up to ₹1 lakh.

## Why this provision is in the corpus

The core provision for "someone used my password / OTP / Aadhaar
details" — read with s. 66D (cheating by personation) and s. 43
(civil compensation).
