# Information Technology Act, 2000 — Section 79: Exemption from liability of intermediary in certain cases

Act: Information Technology Act, 2000 (Act 21 of 2000)
Section: Section 79 (Chapter XII — Intermediaries not to be liable in certain cases)
Source: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/ (India Code); bare Act text at https://www.courtkutchehry.com/bare-acts/information-technology-act-2000/

#it-act #intermediary #safe-harbour #platform #social-media

## Statutory text (summary of the provision, verified)

Section 79 provides that an **intermediary is not liable** for any
third-party information, data or communication link made available
or hosted by it, if:

(a) its function is limited to providing access to a communication
system over which information is transmitted or temporarily stored
or hosted; and

(b) it does not initiate the transmission, select the receiver of
the transmission, or select or modify the information contained in
the transmission; and

(c) it observes due diligence while discharging its duties under the
Act and the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and
Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, and acts expeditiously to remove
or disable access to unlawful material on receiving **actual
knowledge** (including through a court order or a government
notification).

## Plain-language note (summary, not the law)

Section 79 is the "safe harbour": a platform is not automatically
liable for what its users post. The protection is **conditional** —
it is lost if the platform edits or selects the content, fails due
diligence, or does not act on a court order or government notice.
It protects the platform, not the person who posted the content.

## Why this provision is in the corpus

The answer to "can I hold the platform responsible for what someone
posted?" — read with Article 19(2) (what content can lawfully be
restricted) and the *Shreya Singhal* case note.
