# Constitution of India — Article 21: Protection of life and personal liberty

Act: Constitution of India, 1950
Section: Article 21 (Part III — Fundamental Rights)
Source: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/ (India Code); https://sooperkanoon.com/act/133326/constitution-of-india-complete-act

#constitution #fundamental-rights #life-liberty #privacy

## Statutory text (verbatim extract)

> "No person shall be deprived of his life or personal liberty except according to procedure established by law."

## Plain-language note (summary, not the law)

Article 21 protects life and personal liberty for every person, not
only citizens. Unlike Article 19, it is not limited to citizens.
Unlike Article 14, it is not limited to the State's treatment of
equals — it is a guarantee against deprivation of life or liberty
without a lawful procedure.

## Case note (verified)

*Maneka Gandhi v. Union of India* (1978) is the landmark case on
Article 21. It is cited here by name and year only; the corpus does
not reproduce its reasoning.

## Why this provision is in the corpus

The anchor for any question about personal liberty, privacy, or
bodily autonomy — including identity theft and impersonation online,
read with IT Act ss. 66C and 66D.
