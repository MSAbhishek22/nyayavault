## Coverage note

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 is **not in the
NyayaVault corpus**. NyayaVault cannot answer questions about the
DPDP Act, data fiduciaries, consent managers, the Data Protection
Board, or data-breach notification duties under that Act.

For data-related questions, the corpus covers only the Information
Technology Act, 2000 provisions on unauthorised access (section 43),
computer-related offences (section 66), identity theft
(section 66C) and cheating by personation (section 66D).

This note exists so that a question about the DPDP Act gets a
grounded "not covered" answer instead of a guess borrowed from
another Act — for example, the word "breach" also appears in the
Indian Contract Act, 1872 (section 73, breach of contract), which
is a different kind of breach entirely.

## Plain-language note (summary, not the law)

A coverage note is a boundary marker, not a law. It records what
the corpus does not contain, so the app can say so plainly.
