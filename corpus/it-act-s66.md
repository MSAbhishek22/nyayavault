# Information Technology Act, 2000 — Section 66: Computer related offences

Act: Information Technology Act, 2000 (Act 21 of 2000)
Section: Section 66 (Chapter XI — Offences)
Source: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/ (India Code); bare Act text at https://www.courtkutchehry.com/bare-acts/information-technology-act-2000/

#it-act #cyber #hacking #offence #criminal

## Statutory text (summary of the provision, verified)

Section 66 provides that if any person **dishonestly or
fraudulently** does any act referred to in section 43, he shall be
punished with **imprisonment of either description for a term which
may extend to three years, or with fine which may extend to five
lakh rupees, or with both**.

## Plain-language note (summary, not the law)

Section 66 is the criminal version of section 43. The difference is
the mental element: s. 43 is civil liability for the act itself,
while s. 66 requires the act to be done **dishonestly or
fraudulently**. This is the section usually called the "hacking"
section.

## Why this provision is in the corpus

The criminal answer to unauthorised access, data theft and computer
damage — read with s. 43 (civil compensation) and ss. 66C/66D
(identity theft and cheating by personation).
